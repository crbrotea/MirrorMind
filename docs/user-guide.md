# User Guide

MirrorMind is an emotional wellness experience that listens to your voice, detects your emotional state, generates evolving artistic landscapes that reflect how you feel, and guides you toward calm through breathing exercises.

## Session Flow

A MirrorMind session progresses through five stages:

```
Welcome → Mirror → Shift → Arrive → Complete
```

### 1. Landing Page

Open the app and click **"Comenzar"** (Begin) to start a new session. You can also access the gallery of past sessions from the landing page.

### 2. Welcome Stage

The session initializes and connects to the backend. You'll see a brief loading animation while your emotional space is being prepared.

### 3. Mirror Stage (Listening & Reflecting)

This is the core interaction phase:

- **Tap the microphone button** to start speaking
- The app captures your voice in real-time and sends it to the AI agent
- The agent analyzes your tone, words, rhythm, and pauses to detect your emotional state
- Based on the detected emotion, the agent generates an **artistic landscape** that visually reflects how you feel:

| Emotion | Landscape |
|---------|-----------|
| Anxiety | Dense forest with fog, narrow paths, oppressive light |
| Sadness | Vast empty plain, lone tree, rain, twilight |
| Anger | Volcanic terrain, cracked earth, lightning, storm |
| Joy | Wildflower meadow, rolling hills, golden sunlight |
| Calm | Mountain lake at dawn, soft pink-gold gradient |
| Fear | Dark cavern, ice-blue tones, distant light |
| Hope | Forest clearing, morning light, clearing mist |
| Love | Secret garden, roses and gold, warm sunset |

- Your speech and the agent's responses appear as overlaid transcripts that fade after a few seconds
- The art evolves as your emotional state changes, with smooth 3-second crossfade transitions

### 4. Shift Stage (Transformation)

The agent transitions from listening to guiding:

- Asks you how you'd like to feel: *"How would you like to feel?"*
- Offers a **breathing exercise** tailored to your current state
- An animated breathing guide appears on screen with visual cues:
  - **Circle expands** = Inhale
  - **Circle holds** = Hold
  - **Circle contracts** = Exhale
  - **Circle rests** = Rest

#### Breathing Techniques

| Technique | Pattern | Best For |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Physiological Sigh** | Double inhale + long exhale | Anxiety, acute stress |
| **4-7-8 Breathing** | 4s inhale, 7s hold, 8s exhale | Sadness, exhaustion |
| **Box Breathing** | 4s inhale, 4s hold, 4s exhale, 4s rest | General balance, focus |

- As you breathe, the artistic landscape gradually transforms toward calm and warmth
- Colors soften, elements of hope appear, the scene opens up

### 5. Arrive Stage (Destination Reached)

- A final, peaceful landscape is generated representing your emotional destination
- The agent reflects on your journey from where you started to where you are now
- A closing message with warmth and validation

### 6. Complete Stage

- The session ends with a success screen showing your final landscape
- Your emotional journey is saved to the gallery
- You can navigate to the **Gallery** to review your session or return **Home**

## Gallery

The gallery stores all your past sessions. Each entry shows:

- Session date and time
- Thumbnail of the final landscape
- Emotional journey (list of detected emotions)
- Final emotional state
- Number of images generated

Access the gallery from the landing page ("Ver galeria de sesiones") or after completing a session.

## Session Controls

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Microphone button | Tap to start/stop speaking |
| Back arrow (top-left) | Return to home |
| Timer (top-center) | Shows elapsed session time |
| Stage dots (top) | Visual progress through the 5 stages |
| End session (top-right) | Manually end the session early |

## Tips for the Best Experience

1. **Use headphones** to avoid audio feedback between speaker and microphone
2. **Find a quiet space** where you feel comfortable speaking openly
3. **Speak naturally** - the AI detects emotion from tone, rhythm, and pauses, not just words
4. **Follow the breathing guide** during the Shift stage for the best results
5. **Use Chrome** for the best Web Audio API support
6. **Allow microphone access** when prompted by the browser
